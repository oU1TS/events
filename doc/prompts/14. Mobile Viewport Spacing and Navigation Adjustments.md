### Thinking

1. **Diagnose Root Cause of Mobile Viewport Overlap**:
   - **Dynamic Viewport Height (`100vh` vs `100dvh`)**: Mobile Safari and Chrome dynamically show/hide URL bars and navigation controls. Using `height: calc(100vh - 68px)` extends beyond the visible viewport when bottom toolbars are visible, placing `.nav-mobile-auth-wrapper` directly under browser address bars.
   - **Safe-Area Insets**: Devices with home swipe indicators or curved displays need `env(safe-area-inset-bottom)`.
   - **Viewport Meta Missing `viewport-fit=cover`**: Without `viewport-fit=cover`, mobile Safari sets `env(safe-area-inset-bottom)` to `0px`.
   - **Scroll Containment in Drawer**: `.nav` lacked `overflow-y: auto`, which caused buttons to be pushed offscreen on smaller screens or landscape orientations.

2. **Formulate Solution**:
   - Update `<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1.0, viewport-fit=cover">` in `index.html` and `render.html`.
   - In `style.css`:
     - Update `.nav` inside `@media (max-width: 768px)` with `height: calc(100dvh - 68px)`, `overflow-y: auto; -webkit-overflow-scrolling: touch; overscroll-behavior-y: contain;`, `justify-content: flex-start;`, and `padding-bottom: calc(3.5rem + env(safe-area-inset-bottom, 24px))`.
     - Elevate `.nav-mobile-auth-wrapper` with `padding-bottom: calc(2.5rem + env(safe-area-inset-bottom, 24px))` and `margin-bottom: 1.5rem`.
     - Add `min-height: 48px`, tap feedback, and `touch-action: manipulation` to `.nav-mobile-auth-btn`.
     - Provide extra bottom clearance across floating action buttons (`.floating-calendar-btn`, `.floating-past-raids-btn`), `.main-content`, `.footer`, custom modals, and `.toast-container`.
   - Update `documentation.md` (v1.14.0) and `learning.md` (UX Matrix).
