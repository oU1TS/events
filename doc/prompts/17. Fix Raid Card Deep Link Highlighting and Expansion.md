But currently it seems to flicker and show the highlight slow halfway before glitching and retracting to the default card view.

### Thinking Process

1. **Root Cause Analysis**:
   - Analyzed `app.js` routing and deep link lifecycle when opening `#raid-<num>`.
   - On page load, `loadPastRaids()` renders the raid cards and triggers `scrollToRaid()`, which expands `<details class="parent-raid-card">` and adds the 2-second `.highlighted` animation (`highlightPulse`).
   - Simultaneously, `initSupabaseAuth()` runs asynchronously in the background (`supabaseClient.auth.getSession()` and `auth.onAuthStateChange`).
   - In `syncAuthState(session)`, when unauthenticated (`!session || !session.user`), it unconditionally invoked `filterAndRenderRaids()`, completely clearing `raidsContainer.innerHTML = ''` ~300ms to ~700ms after initial render.
   - This DOM wipe destroyed the active `<details>` element midway through the highlight animation and recreated it in its default unexpanded, unhighlighted state—causing the observed flicker, mid-animation glitch, and snap retraction back to collapsed view.
   - In addition, `renderRaids` did not remember previously open cards or target hash cards upon re-rendering.
2. **Solution Design**:
   - Guard `syncAuthState` to only re-render if the user's role or auth identity actually changed (`roleChanged`).
   - In `renderRaids()`, record currently opened card IDs (`details[open]`) before clearing container and ensure open states and active highlights are preserved across re-renders.
   - Optimize `routePage()` to invoke `scrollToRaid` directly when cards are already loaded in the DOM instead of destroying and re-rendering all cards.
   - Harden `scrollToRaid` with debounce timer, forced reflow for animation restart, and fallback cleanup.
3. **Dual-Write Documentation**:
   - Created implementation plan artifact `implementation_plan.md` and dual-wrote to `doc/prompts/17.1 Implementation Plan - Raid Card Deep Link Highlighting & Retraction Fix.md`.
